# UNFORGETTABLE (2019) 🎬

**Unforgettable** is a heartwarming Filipino movie starring *Sarah Geronimo* and an adorable dog named **Milo**.

## Movie Description
The story highlights the special bond between humans and animals. It shows how dogs bring happiness, comfort, and unconditional love into our lives.

## Why I Love This Movie ❤️
- I am an animal lover.
- It reminds me that **dogs are family, not just pets**.
- It teaches us the importance of kindness, loyalty, and compassion.

## My Favorite Lesson
> "The love of a dog is unconditional, and the memories we create with them last forever."

**Overall:** Unforgettable is one of my favorite movies because it reminds me to appreciate and love animals. 🐶
